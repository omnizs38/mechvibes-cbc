About Mechvibes

Mechvibes started as a passion project to bring the satisfying sound of mechanical keyboards to every Windows user. What began in 2021 has grown into a comprehensive keyboard sound engine trusted by thousands of users worldwide.

Whether you're a competitive gamer, a developer, or just someone who loves the feel and sound of mechanical keyboards, Mechvibes delivers an authentic experience with zero compromises.

Built with modern technologies and optimized for performance, Mechvibes continues to evolve with community feedback and contributions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Mechvibes free?

Yes, Mechvibes is completely free and open source under the MPL-2.0 license.

Does it work with all keyboards?

Mechvibes works with any keyboard connected to Windows. The sounds play from your PC speakers or headphones.

Will it affect game performance?

No. Mechvibes is optimized for minimal resource usage and won't impact your gaming performance.

Can I customize the sounds?

Yes! Mechvibes offers extensive customization options for sound profiles, volume, and effects.

How do I report bugs?

Report issues on our GitHub Issues page. Include your system info and steps to reproduce the bug.

Can I contribute?

Absolutely! We welcome contributions. Check our CONTRIBUTING guide on GitHub for details.
