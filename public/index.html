Built for mechanical keyboard enthusiasts

This is a fork of Mechvibes, the mechanical keyboard sound engine Hai Nguyen released in 2021 under the MPL-2.0. It keeps the idea and rebuilds the software: a TypeScript codebase on a current Electron runtime, a reworked low-latency audio path, and a native v4 soundpack format.

It is a young project and the numbers below say exactly where it stands - no rounding up. Everything is open source, so read the code, open an issue, or fork it again.
